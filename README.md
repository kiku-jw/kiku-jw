## Apps & tools

- **[Say More!](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6803824561)**: conversation cards for friends and groups.
- **[FitKiku](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6801516904)**: share your steps and sleep with your AI assistant.
- **[Page Media Downloader](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/page-media-downloader/gipfohoiellkikkpbhhafjodamlfikod)**: save images and directly available media from web pages.
- **[ClipStash](https://github.com/kiku-jw/ClipStash)**: find and reuse text and images you copied on your Mac.
- **[Kyuva](https://github.com/kiku-jw/kyuva)**: read your script near the camera while recording or presenting on your Mac.
